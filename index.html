How to set your own reminders
Google Calendar
  1. On a phone? Subscribe once from a computer — it then syncs to the Google Calendar app.
  2. Find "SNYP" under Other calendars.
  3. Click ⋮ → Settings and sharing.
  4. Under Event notifications, add the reminders you want.
iPhone / Mac
  1. Tap Apple / iPhone and confirm Subscribe.
  2. iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars → SNYP → turn off Remove Alerts.
  3. Mac: Calendar → Settings → Alerts.
Outlook
  1. Confirm the subscription when Outlook opens. (Work/school account? use this link.)
  2. Right-click the SNYP calendar → Settings.
  3. Pick your default reminder time.
